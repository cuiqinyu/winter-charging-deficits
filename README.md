# Hidden winter deficits in public electric vehicle charging infrastructure

Materials that accompany the manuscript *Hidden winter deficits in public electric vehicle charging infrastructure* (under review).

## Contents

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `calculator/` | Interactive calculator: how many more public chargers a country or city needs on its coldest days. One self-contained web page, `calculator/index.html`; open it in any browser. |

More materials will be added here as the manuscript moves forward.

## Calculator

Choose a country or region, or search for a city. The page shows

- the driving range of a reference electric vehicle in mild weather and on the coldest days of the year,
- the additional public charging needed on those days, and the number of additional public chargers,
- how much access to home charging could reduce that.

The second view of the page, "Methods and data sources", explains the calculation and lists the source of the electric vehicle stock of each place. The page is available in English and Chinese.
